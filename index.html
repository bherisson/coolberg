The Coolberg story: India
Origin

A different kind of beer moment

Coolberg started with a simple question: why should flavour, ritual and refreshment require alcohol at all? Built in India as a malt-based beverage at 0.0% ABV.

Idea

Zero alcohol, by design

0.0% isn't a "diet" version of something else, it's the whole point. Every recipe is built from the ground up for full flavour at zero alcohol.

Range

Seven flavours, one identity

From classic malt to bold ginger and bright cranberry, a range built to fit every craving, staying unmistakably Coolberg.

Ship's log: Coolberg in Mauritius
01
Arrival

Landing on the island

Coolberg's arrival in Mauritius wasn't a corporate rollout. It followed the island's own rhythm: first into bars and restaurants in Grand Baie and Port Louis, the way most good things arrive here.

02
Today

Made for Mauritian moments

Now on supermarket shelves across the country, from Sunday table d'hôte lunches to weekend gatherings in Flic-en-Flac, and evenings that don't need alcohol to be memorable.

03
Next

Across the island, region by region

From the North's beach towns to the Central Plateau's cooler evenings, the plan is simple: everywhere Mauritius gathers, Coolberg should be within reach.
